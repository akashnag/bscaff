# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We want bscaff to be a welcoming, respectful, and constructive open-source project.

We welcome contributions from people with different backgrounds, experience levels, perspectives, and approaches. Everyone participating in the project is expected to help maintain a professional and respectful environment.

## Expected Behaviour

Contributors and community members are expected to:

* Be respectful and considerate in discussions and reviews.
* Assume good intentions while remaining open to correcting misunderstandings.
* Focus criticism on ideas, code, documentation, and decisions rather than individuals.
* Provide constructive and actionable feedback.
* Be willing to consider perspectives different from their own.
* Respect differing levels of experience and technical knowledge.
* Keep discussions relevant to the project.
* Accept that maintainers may make decisions about the project's direction after considering community feedback.
* Respect the project's contribution guidelines and technical standards.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviour is not acceptable:

* Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
* Threats, intimidation, or deliberately hostile behaviour.
* Trolling, sustained disruption, or intentionally derailing discussions.
* Insults or derogatory comments directed at individuals or groups.
* Publishing someone's private or personal information without their permission.
* Sexual harassment or unwanted sexual attention.
* Deliberate impersonation or misrepresentation of another contributor.
* Repeatedly ignoring reasonable requests from maintainers or moderators.
* Any other behaviour that would reasonably make participation unsafe or hostile.

## Technical Disagreements

Technical disagreement is a normal and valuable part of open-source development.

Contributors are encouraged to challenge implementations, designs, assumptions, and proposed decisions. Such disagreement should remain focused on the technical subject rather than becoming personal.

A rejected proposal is not a rejection of the person proposing it.

Maintainers may make final decisions when consensus cannot reasonably be reached, particularly where decisions concern project scope, architecture, maintainability, or release requirements.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for addressing behaviour that violates this Code of Conduct.

Depending on the circumstances, maintainers may:

* Ask a participant to modify their behaviour.
* Remove inappropriate comments or content.
* Close discussions, issues, or pull requests that become abusive or disruptive.
* Temporarily restrict participation.
* Permanently restrict participation in the project.

Enforcement decisions will take the circumstances and severity of the behaviour into account.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behaviour that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it privately to the project maintainers rather than escalating the situation publicly.

Use the project's designated private contact or security/reporting channel where one is provided.

When reporting an incident, please provide as much relevant information as you can, including:

* What happened
* When and where it happened
* Who was involved
* Links or other relevant evidence, if available

Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces and interactions where an individual is representing or participating in the bscaff community, including:

* GitHub issues and pull requests
* Discussions
* Code reviews
* Project-related communication channels
* Other project-sponsored or project-related spaces

Maintainers may also take action regarding behaviour outside these spaces when it has a direct and significant impact on the safety or ability of community members to participate in the project.

## Good-Faith Participation

This Code of Conduct is intended to support a healthy and productive community, not to police ordinary technical disagreement.

Reports should be made in good faith. Deliberately false or malicious reports may themselves be considered a violation of this Code of Conduct.

## Acknowledgement

By participating in the bscaff project, you agree to follow this Code of Conduct and help maintain a respectful and constructive community.
